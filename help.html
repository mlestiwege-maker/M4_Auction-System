Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about AuctionHub

Getting Started

How do I create an account? ▼

Click the "Create Account" button on our homepage or go to the registration page. Fill in your email, username, and password, then click submit. You'll immediately be able to start bidding!

Is registration free? ▼

Yes! Creating an account on AuctionHub is completely free. There are no hidden fees for registration or browsing auctions. You only pay when you win an item.

How do I log in? ▼

Simply enter your username and password on the login page. If you forgot your password, contact our support team. Make sure your browser allows cookies and JavaScript for the best experience.

Bidding & Auctions

How do I place a bid? ▼

When viewing an auction, enter your bid amount in the bidding form and click "Place Bid". Your bid must be higher than the current highest bid. You'll see an instant confirmation.

What is auto-bidding? ▼

Auto-bidding allows our system to bid on your behalf up to a maximum price you set. If someone outbids you, we'll automatically place a new bid (up to your maximum) to keep you in the lead.

How long do auctions last? ▼

Auction length is set by the seller when they create the item. Most auctions last between 3 to 30 days. You can see the exact end time on each auction page with a live countdown timer.

What happens if I'm outbid? ▼

You'll receive a notification if you're outbid. If you have auto-bidding enabled, our system will automatically place a higher bid for you (up to your maximum). If the bid exceeds your maximum, you can place a new manual bid.

What does the Watchlist do? ▼

The Watchlist lets you save your favorite auctions for later. You can add items to your watchlist by clicking the star icon. You'll be able to quickly access them from your watchlist page.

Selling Items

How do I list an item for auction? ▼

Log in to your account and navigate to "Sell Item" or your seller dashboard. Fill in the item details (title, description, category, starting price, images), set the duration, and submit. Your item will be live immediately!

Can I edit or delete my listings? ▼

Yes! From your seller dashboard, you can edit your active auctions to change the description, images, or other details. If no bids have been placed yet, you can also delete the item entirely.

How many items can I sell? ▼

There's no limit! Sell as many items as you'd like. Build your reputation through successful auctions and positive reviews to attract more buyers.

How do I see my earnings? ▼

Your seller dashboard displays your total earnings from completed auctions. You can see metrics including the number of items sold, total bids received, and estimated earnings.

Reviews & Ratings

How do I leave a review? ▼

You can leave a review for a seller after you've won one of their auctions. Go to the auction page and fill in the review form (1-5 stars and optional comments). Your review helps other buyers make informed decisions.

Can I see all reviews for a seller? ▼

Yes! Each auction page displays the seller's average rating and shows recent reviews. Click "View all seller reviews" to see the complete history of feedback for that seller.

Notifications

What are notifications? ▼

Notifications keep you updated on your auctions. You'll receive alerts when you're outbid, when your auction is about to end, when you win an item, and when a new bid is placed on your auction.

Where can I see all my notifications? ▼

Click the notification bell icon in the header to see your recent notifications. You can also go to your notification center for a complete list of all past notifications.
