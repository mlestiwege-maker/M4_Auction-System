Your privacy is important to us

We only use your information to run the marketplace, support bidding, and improve your experience. No drama, no hidden tricks.

Last updated: May 2026

AuctionHub ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AuctionHub marketplace. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your username, email address, password, and profile information.
  • Auction Information: Information about items you list, bids you place, and auctions you participate in.
  • Communication Data: Messages, reviews, ratings, and feedback you provide through our platform.
  • Technical Information: IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and usage patterns through cookies and similar technologies.

2. Use of Data

AuctionHub uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To monitor usage of our Service
  • To detect, prevent and address technical issues and fraud

3. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

4. Cookies

Our Service uses cookies (small files stored on your device) to enhance your experience. Cookies allow us to recognize you, remember your preferences, and understand how you use our platform. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may limit your ability to use certain features.

Our Service may contain links to other sites that are not operated by us. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies or practices of any third-party sites or services.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "effective date" at the top of this Privacy Policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our contact page for help.
